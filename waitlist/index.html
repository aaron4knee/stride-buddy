Why it's different

Made for desk days, not marathon training

5-minute breaks, not 10,000 steps

A walk to the kitchen counts. So do stairs, stretches or a lap of the block between meetings.

A buddy that never guilts you

Pip grows or rests. It never shrinks, gets sick or sends a sad notification. Missed days are rest days.

Built to be outgrown

Ninety days to build the habit, then a graduation and a keepsake. No subscription to forget about.

How it works

Three steps, most of them on your wrist

  1. Hatch your egg

    Pick a buddy and a daily goal (5 breaks to start). Your first day's breaks hatch the egg.

  2. Move 5 minutes

    After 30 minutes of sitting, a quiet tap. Any 5 minutes of movement counts as a break.

  3. Grow, then graduate

    On-track weeks unlock new forms and gear. On day 90, your buddy moves out and you keep the habit.

The research

In a small Columbia University lab study, about 5 minutes of light movement every 30 minutes of sitting cut blood-sugar spikes after meals by 58% and lifted mood. Stride Buddy turns that rhythm into something you'll want to keep doing.

Duran, Diaz et al., Columbia University, 2023. A research finding, not a guarantee. Not medical advice.
